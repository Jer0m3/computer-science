# SANS SEC555 – Detection Engineering and SIEM Analytics (GIAC GCDA)

**Status:** 🟡 in preparation

**Course link:** <https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-courses/detection-engineering-siem-analytics>

**Certification:** [GIAC Certified Detection Analyst (GCDA)](https://www.giac.org/certifications/certified-detection-analyst-gcda/)

## Exam facts

- 1 proctored exam, 75 questions, 2 hours
- Passing score: 76% (exam versions released on or after 2026-04-27)
- Open book: build a good index of the course books before the exam
- Check the official GCDA page for the current exam objectives before booking

## Study plan

1. Work through one section per week: read the book, then do all labs for it.
2. Write index entries while reading (term → book/page), not afterwards.
3. Repeat the labs in a home lab (e.g. Wazuh or Elastic) with your own data.
4. Take practice test 1, close the gaps, update the index.
5. Take practice test 2 about one week before the exam.
6. Book the exam.

## Free material to go with it

- [Sigma rules](https://github.com/SigmaHQ/sigma) – vendor-neutral detection rules, good for practicing detection logic
- [Splunk Boss of the SOC datasets](https://github.com/splunk/botsv3) – real-world log data for hunting and alerting practice
- [Atomic Red Team](https://github.com/redcanaryco/atomic-red-team) – generate test activity in your lab and check if your detections fire
- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) – map each detection to a technique
